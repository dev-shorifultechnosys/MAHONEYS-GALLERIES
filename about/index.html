Our story

Mahoneys Galleries is a place to look closely, ask questions and build a relationship with art.

From its Brighton space, the gallery brings contemporary work into a setting designed for considered viewing, thoughtful conversation and genuine discovery.

Artists, collectors and visitors meet through a programme shaped around the work itself, with personal guidance available for those looking to learn, collect or simply spend more time with art.

Brighton · Melbourne
